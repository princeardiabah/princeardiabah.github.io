KNUST Faculty of Law · Ghana

Academic Integrity Checker

The Problem: Checking every emailed assignment for plagiarism by hand is slow, and students rarely know whether their submission even arrived.

The Build: An end-to-end n8n system: students email their work, it is logged with a tracking number, the PDF text is extracted and compared with every earlier submission by an AI integrity check, and the result is routed automatically.

The Output: An instant receipt for the student, a cleared or flagged outcome on every submission, and an alert to the professor only when work is flagged.

The Result: Lecturers review the cases that need judgement instead of reading every paper for overlap, and every submission leaves an audit trail.

  1. Email in
  2. Tracking number + receipt
  3. PDF text extracted
  4. Compared with past submissions
  5. AI integrity verdict
  6. Cleared or flagged
  7. Professor alerted if flagged
