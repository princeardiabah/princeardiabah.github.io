Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Many people I work with have never built an automation. We start from your business and pick tools you can actually maintain.

Which tools do we use?

Usually n8n, OpenAI or Groq, Supabase, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, and whatever your team already relies on.

What do you need to plan a talk?

The date, the audience, the format and what you want people to leave with. I’ll suggest a talk or workshop that fits.

What if I just want you to build it for me?

That’s what my automation work is for. See the systems I build or book a call and we’ll scope it.
